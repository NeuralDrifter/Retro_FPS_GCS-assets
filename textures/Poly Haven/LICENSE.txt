These textures are from Poly Haven (https://polyhaven.com/textures).

License: CC0 (Public Domain)
https://polyhaven.com/license

Each file is the 1K diffuse (color) map of the Poly Haven texture with the same name,
for example brick_floor_04.webp is https://polyhaven.com/a/brick_floor_04.
They were downscaled to 256x256 and converted to WebP; colors are unchanged.
The normal, roughness and displacement maps were not included.
